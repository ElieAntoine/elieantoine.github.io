Elie Antoine

Postdoctoral researcher in NLP at RALI-DIRO, Université de Montréal.

I am a postdoctoral researcher in Natural Language Processing at RALI-DIRO, Université de Montréal, working with Jian-Yun Nie in the framework of the CLIQ-ai consortium.

I completed my PhD in NLP at Aix-Marseille Université, in the TALEP team at the Laboratoire d'Informatique et des Systèmes (LIS), under the supervision of Frédéric Béchet (LIS) and Philippe Langlais (DIRO, UdeM).

My research focuses on the assessment of language models, by which I mean understanding what they actually do and where their limits lie, rather than only measuring aggregate performance. I have explored this through several directions during my PhD: linguistically-motivated evaluation of reading comprehension, the analysis of routing patterns in Mixture-of-Experts architectures, and, within the Archival project, user-centered evaluation and explainable document linking for digital humanities collections.
